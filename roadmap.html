Research architecture

From gauge configurations to the claimed mass gap

This is a reading guide, not an independent verification. The arrows represent the principal proposed dependencies of the construction. The core Yang–Mills/collective-field correspondence is particularly important: the mathematical construction of a positive Gaussian field theory alone does not establish that it is the large-N limit of Yang–Mills without that identification.

Scope of the claims. A node labeled “claimed theorem” describes a result asserted by the manuscript, not a theorem independently certified by referees. The conifold reconstruction relies on the period/RG and color-cotangent identifications stated in the text. The string interpretation is a separate layer, not a prerequisite for the spectral proof.
