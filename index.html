Overview

The research program

This manuscript proposes a planar Yang–Mills master-field formulation built from a large-N color-algebra limit, renormalization-group completion, and an associated geometric spectral problem. It develops a reflection-positive Gaussian fluctuation construction within the stated master-field identification and analyzes the resulting mass-gap operator.

Research status: This is a publicly shared research manuscript, not a peer-reviewed publication. The scope and assumptions of its principal existence claim are discussed in the full text. Readers are invited to scrutinize the derivations.

Yang–Mills and the planar field

Gauge-reduced configuration space, fuzzy-sphere color algebra, and the collective map to geometric variables.

02

RG and emergent geometry

Conifold periods, Stenzel geometry, and the radial Poisson problem defining the physical Riesz weight.

03

Spectral analysis

Constrained Dolbeault complex, warped Gauss slice, one-sided mass operator, and glueball channels.

04

Quantum measure and reconstruction

Planar Gaussian covariance, Osterwalder–Schrader properties, and the decompactification framework.
